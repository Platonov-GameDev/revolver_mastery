# FPS-Slasher genre mixing prototype

https://youtu.be/-x5AYZd-zcY?si=Ny9-bM8xBC6NgAve

An experiment in blending the shooter mechanics with a slasher-like deep control scheme with multiple abilities triggered by different button combinations.

## General rules

General goal of the game is to get as high of a score as possible, where score is based on the KPM (kills per minute) value.

There is a "heat" gauge, which quickly fills when dealing damage using different moves, not spamming one move only.
With each filled bar of the heat gauge, the speed of player animations increases, allowing to deal damage more quickly, while also requiring to execute moves faster.

Each action kicks player up in the air a bit, allowing player to stay airborne if moves are smoothly executed without pause.

Different moves have different special effects:
- Fan pierces walls and marks enemies, making them take double damage and visible through walls
- Auto ricochets off of surfaces, auto-targeting enemies a little bit
- Shotgun kicks player back and stuns nearby enemies for a brief period
- Grenade/Blast kicks enemies and player up in the air
- Chain Pull pulls all enemies in the hit area towards a single point
- Pierce just damages everything in a big cone, piercing both enemies and walls
